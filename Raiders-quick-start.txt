Getting started

1. In Windows, right-click the ZIP, choose Extract All, and open
   Reverse's Raid Mode Raiders.exe from the extracted folder as your normal
   Windows user. The first launch scans running and installed apps.
2. Discord and World of Warcraft start Rostered. Other eligible apps start
   Benched, including apps first launched during a raid. Windows protections
   stay on. In App Roster, right-click an app to change its rule. You can also
   check several apps and choose Roster, Bench, or Flex above the table.
   Flex apps stay open unless you enable closing Flex apps in Details.
3. Choose Scan & preview and review the Action column. Apply suggestions
   rosters detected Battle.net and Archon apps, your Windows default browser
   and other running browsers the scan recognizes. Apps you explicitly Bench
   or set to Flex are skipped. Use Ctrl-click or Shift-click to edit a group.
4. Choose Start raid to see the list of apps scheduled to close. Save your
   work first. At the bottom of this screen, choose how to start the raid:
   - Start session & resume my apps afterward. When the raid ends, Raid Mode
     reopens desktop apps it confirmed closed at the start. Apps that support
     restoring a session can bring back their tabs or documents. Other apps
     open normally. Unsaved work depends on the app.
   - Start session & leave my apps closed. You can open them yourself whenever
     you're ready.
   - Start session & use my per-app choices. Raid Mode follows the After raid
     choice you saved for each app. Apps without a choice stay closed.
   The first two buttons override the individual choices for this raid.
   Cancel takes you back to your preview. You choose each time, and Resume
   is highlighted even if you chose something different last time.
5. Choose End session to stop monitoring, restore paused startup entries and
   carry out your choice. Apps already running are left alone. Raid Mode
   also recognizes tray apps that have a desktop launcher or register for
   restart with Windows. Apps first launched during a raid and individual
   background helpers are excluded from automatic reopening. A window waiting
   for you to save stays open. See Activity for individual results.

Where your rules go

Changes save automatically to your Windows account as soon as you edit a rule.
You can close the app immediately and see your choices the next time you open
it. To find your settings, paste this into the File Explorer address bar:

    %LOCALAPPDATA%\RaidModeRaiders

The file whitelist.json holds BOTH your Rostered and Benched lists. Moving
the Raid Mode app or extracting another copy uses the same saved profile.
To save an individual return preference, right-click an app in App Roster
and choose After raid. You can set several selected apps together. Stay
closed, Start fresh and Resume if supported apply when you start a raid
with Use my per-app choices. Start fresh opens a new window in supported
apps. Other apps follow their own startup settings.
Rostered and Benched rules follow an executable into a new version folder in its
installation. If an update moves it elsewhere, Raid Mode can also recognize
an app by its executable name, publisher and product once the old file is gone.
Shared helpers with generic names need a matching installation path. If Raid
Mode cannot identify a move, the new entry follows your raid defaults.
Click Details beside the updated app to see which saved rule it matched.
Clear rule sets the selected app to Flex. Manage rules lets you turn raid
defaults off or on. Clear all removes raid rules and turns off the defaults.
